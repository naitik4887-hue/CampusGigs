How it works

You can be up and running in a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Sign up with your college email

    We send a 6-digit code to confirm the address is really yours.

  2. 2

    Post or browse

    Offer a service, ask for help, or list an item with photos. Search and filter to find what you need.

  3. 3

    Message and meet on campus

    Chat inside the app, agree on details, and meet in a public spot. Pay each other directly.

Built to keep it students only

A campus community works when everyone in it is who they say they are.

College email required

Only college email domains can sign up, and every account confirms its address with a one-time code.

ID check for the Marketplace

To buy or sell items, you verify your college ID card once. A reviewer approves it.

Your ID photo is deleted

The photo is only seen by a reviewer and is deleted as soon as the review is done.

Private messaging

Chat happens in the app, so you never have to share your email or phone number.

Questions people ask

Who can join?

Students with a valid college email address. Buying and selling in the Marketplace also needs a verified college ID.

Is it free?

Yes. Posting, browsing and messaging cost nothing.

How do payments work?

CampusGigs doesn't handle money. You agree on the price in chat and pay each other directly when you meet, so meet in a public place on campus and check items before paying.

What happens to my ID photo?

It is used only to check your name and college, then deleted once the review is finished. We keep just your ID number and the decision, to stop one card being used on many accounts.

Can I use it with another college's students?

Yes. Students from any allowed college can use the site, and you can filter listings to only your own college.
